# DrivenCare, Medical Scheduling

An appointment scheduling API. The patient books with doctors across specialities by picking a free date and time, and the doctor confirms or cancels.

I modelled the schedule so two patients cannot take the same slot, and kept the appointment history visible to both sides. JWT authentication and Joi validation, over PostgreSQL.

It was my first project in healthcare. I ended up back on the same ground later, on the clinical platform at Tiix.
