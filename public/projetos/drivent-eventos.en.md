# Driven.t, Event Manager

An API for event management: registration, tickets, accommodation, room selection and activities by track.

I wrote the back-end in TypeScript with Express and Prisma over PostgreSQL, splitting route, service and repository into layers.

The business rules take more work than they look like they would. The ticket type decides whether there is a hotel, the hotel decides which rooms appear, and availability shifts as other people book.

I was also the team's Scrum Master, organising the workload and the deliveries.
